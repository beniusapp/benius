Please update the Admin Portal → Fees & Payments → Ledger & Transactions table.

I want to make Payment Method a completely separate column from Status.

Current problem

Currently, when an invoice is paid, the Ledger shows something like:

Status
🟢 Paid
"Cash"

This mixes two different pieces of information.

- Status should tell us the financial state of the invoice.
- Payment Method should tell us how the payment was made.

I want these to be completely separated.

---

1. Add a separate Payment Method column

Add a new column named:

Payment Method

The Ledger should display payment methods such as:

- Cash
- Cheque
- Bank Transfer
- Demand Draft
- UPI / QR
- Portal Payment

Important

For payments made through the Student Portal using the online payment gateway, the Payment Method should be:

Portal Payment

Do NOT use:

- Online
- Digital Payment
- Razorpay

as the main Ledger payment-method label.

"Portal Payment" is the agreed terminology for this application.

---

2. Keep Status completely separate

The Status column must only represent the invoice/fee status.

The supported invoice statuses are:

- Due
- Overdue
- Paid

Do not put payment methods underneath the Status badge anymore.

For example:

Correct

Status| Payment Method
Paid| Cash
Paid| Cheque
Paid| Bank Transfer
Paid| Demand Draft
Paid| UPI / QR
Paid| Portal Payment
Due| —
Overdue| —

The "—" means no payment has been made yet, therefore there is no payment method.

---

3. Portal Payment

For a successful payment made from the Student Portal through Razorpay:

Ledger:

Status → "Paid"

Payment Method → "Portal Payment"

Do not display "Razorpay" as the payment method in the Ledger.

Razorpay is the payment gateway, not the user-facing payment method.

The detailed Payment Details section can continue showing the actual gateway information, such as:

- Payment Method: Portal Payment
- Gateway: Razorpay
- Gateway Method: UPI / Card / Net Banking
- Payment ID
- Order ID
- Other existing payment details

Do not remove any existing detailed payment information.

---

4. Offline payments

Make sure the existing offline payment methods map correctly to the new Payment Method column:

- Cash → "Cash"
- Cheque → "Cheque"
- Bank Transfer → "Bank Transfer"
- Demand Draft → "Demand Draft"
- UPI / QR Payment → "UPI / QR"
- Student Portal/Razorpay payment → "Portal Payment"

Use the existing payment data/source wherever possible. Do not duplicate payment records just to implement this UI change.

---

5. Payment failures and cancellations

Do NOT put "Failed" or "Cancelled" into the Payment Method column.

Payment attempts remain a separate concept.

For example:

Invoice:

- Status = "Due"
- Payment Method = "—"

Payment history:

- Attempt #1 = Failed
- Attempt #2 = Cancelled
- Attempt #3 = Captured

After successful capture:

Invoice:

- Status = "Paid"
- Payment Method = "Portal Payment"

Keep the existing payment-attempt/history architecture unchanged.

---

6. Database/backend consistency

Please inspect the existing implementation before making changes.

Do not create duplicate or conflicting payment-method fields if an appropriate field already exists.

Make sure the Ledger API/backend provides the correct payment method for each fee record.

The frontend should not guess the payment method based only on the status.

The value must come from the actual payment/transaction data.

For invoices that have not been paid:

Payment Method = "—"

For paid invoices:

Display the actual method used for that successful payment.

---

7. Existing data

Do not lose any existing payment history.

Do not modify payment amounts, invoice numbers, receipt numbers, payment IDs, order IDs, or payment-attempt records.

If existing paid records already have payment-method information, map them correctly into the new column.

If a historical record genuinely has no payment-method information available, display "—" rather than guessing.

---

8. UI/UX

Keep the existing Ledger & Transactions design, styling, responsiveness, pagination, filters, and overall layout intact.

Only introduce the new Payment Method column and remove the payment-method text currently displayed underneath the Status badge.

The Status badge should contain only the status.

Example:

Status

🟢 Paid

Payment Method

"Portal Payment"

Do not show:

🟢 Paid
"Portal Payment"

inside the Status column.

---

9. Check mobile layout

This is important because the Admin Portal is also used on mobile.

After adding the column, verify that the Ledger remains usable on mobile without breaking the existing horizontal scrolling/table behavior.

Do not shrink the text excessively just to force every column into the screen.

---

10. Final verification

After implementing the change, test at least these cases:

1. Paid by Cash
2. Paid by Cheque
3. Paid by Bank Transfer
4. Paid by Demand Draft
5. Paid by UPI / QR
6. Paid through Student Portal/Razorpay
7. Due invoice
8. Overdue invoice
9. Failed Portal payment attempt
10. Cancelled Portal payment attempt

Expected result:

- Status shows only "Due", "Overdue", or "Paid".
- Payment Method shows the actual successful payment method.
- Failed/Cancelled attempts do not become invoice statuses.
- Unpaid invoices show "—" for Payment Method.
- Student Portal payments show Portal Payment.
- Existing payment history remains unchanged.

Do not make unrelated changes to the Fees & Payments module.
