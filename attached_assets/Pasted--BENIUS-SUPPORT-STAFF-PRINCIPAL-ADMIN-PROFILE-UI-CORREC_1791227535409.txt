# PRODUCT DECISION

Support Staff logs in through the Principal/Admin portal and works on modules granted by Principal.

However:

> Principal/Admin Profile and account settings belong ONLY to Principal/Admin.

Support Staff must NOT have access to the Principal/Admin Profile interface.

---

When Support Staff logs in, the top-right header correctly displays the Support Staff's own identity, for example:


- Profile
- School
- Password
- PIN
- Log
- Recovery Email
- Recovery Phone
- account/PIN information
- Save Changes
- other Principal/Admin account controls

This is incorrect for Support Staff.

- school name

`userRole === "support_staff"`
