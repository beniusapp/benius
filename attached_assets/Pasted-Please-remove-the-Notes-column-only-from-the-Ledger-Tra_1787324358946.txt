Please remove the Notes column only from the Ledger & Transactions table UI.

Current situation

The Notes column in:

Fees & Payments → Ledger & Transactions

currently shows "—" for the records because the existing "fee_records.notes" values are NULL.

The investigation confirmed that there is no data-loss bug. The Notes field is simply not being used/populated for these ledger records.

Required change

Remove the Notes column completely from the Ledger & Transactions table.

This means:

- Remove the Notes column header.
- Remove the Notes cell from every ledger row.
- Remove the "rec.notes || "—"" rendering from this table.
- Reclaim the horizontal space used by the Notes column.
- Adjust column widths if necessary so the remaining columns use the available space properly.

IMPORTANT — Do NOT delete Notes from the system

Do NOT:

- Drop/remove "fee_records.notes" from the database.
- Remove Notes from the invoice creation form.
- Remove Notes from invoice generation logic.
- Remove Notes from receipts.
- Remove Notes from any existing API/database schema.
- Remove Notes from any other module.
- Change the existing invoice or receipt design.
- Change payment functionality.
- Change the Ledger data/API unnecessarily.

The requirement is UI-only for the Ledger & Transactions table.

After removing the column

Use the recovered horizontal space to improve the readability of the remaining important columns.

The Ledger should clearly prioritize:

Fee Name | Fee Type | Amount | Due Date | Status | Payment Method | Paid On | Academic Year | Actions

Do not introduce another unnecessary column just to fill the space.

Verification

After making the change:

1. Open Fees & Payments → Ledger & Transactions.
2. Confirm the Notes column is completely gone.
3. Confirm all existing ledger records still appear.
4. Confirm Paid and Due records are unaffected.
5. Confirm Receipt/Invoice buttons still work.
6. Confirm no database schema or payment logic was changed.
7. Confirm the Notes field still exists and works wherever it is currently used outside this table.

This is a small UI cleanup only. Do not make any unrelated changes.
