# WHAT I WANT TO VERIFY

Support Staff is created by the Principal/Admin of a school.

Support Staff works as an operational assistant to that Principal/Admin.

Therefore:

> Whatever an authorized Support Staff member does inside a module granted by Principal/Admin must operate on the SAME school data used by Principal/Admin.

Support Staff must NOT have a separate copy of school operational data.

---

# SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Principal/Admin of School A grants:

**Fees & Payments → Fee Structures**

Support Staff logs in and creates a Fee Structure.

Expected:

```text id="pgnwub"
Support Staff
     ↓
Creates Fee Structure
     ↓
School A shared database/data
     ↓
Principal/Admin logs in
     ↓
Principal sees the SAME Fee Structure
```

There should NOT be:

```text id="p3kxjz"
Principal Fees Data
       ≠
Support Staff Fees Data
```

---

# IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY

Do NOT interpret “save in Principal portal” as saving data under the Principal user's personal account ID.

The intended architecture is:

> Data belongs to the SCHOOL/TENANT and, where applicable, the Academic Session.

Principal/Admin and authorized Support Staff are two authenticated users operating on that SAME school data.

Support Staff actions should remain attributable to the Support Staff actor where audit/history information exists.

In other words:

```text id="c5dzgt"
DATA OWNER/SCOPE:
School A

ACTION PERFORMED BY:
Support Staff X

VISIBLE TO:
Principal/Admin of School A
and other properly authorized users
```

Do NOT rewrite Support Staff actions to falsely appear as though Principal personally performed them.

---

# MAIN AUDIT QUESTION

When Support Staff performs an authorized action today:

1. Is the resulting data written to the SAME tables/records/storage used by Principal/Admin?
2. Is it scoped to the SAME authenticated school?
3. Is the result immediately available to Principal/Admin through the normal Principal/Admin module?
4. Is there any separate Support Staff-only operational dataset?
5. Does any Support Staff route save data in a way that Principal/Admin cannot subsequently see?
6. Does any Support Staff operation incorrectly associate records with the negative Support Staff `userId` where a school/Admin ownership ID is expected?
7. Are creator/audit fields correctly recording the actual Support Staff actor without changing school ownership?

# CHECK FOR BAD PATTERNS

Specifically look for:

### A. Separate Support Staff data

Support Staff action writes somewhere different from Principal operational data.

### B. Principal cannot see Staff-created data

Staff successfully creates something but normal Principal query excludes it because of creator/user filtering.

### C. Incorrect ownership

Record is owned by Support Staff user rather than the school where school ownership is intended.

### D. Negative-ID problems

Support Staff's negative compatibility `userId` is written into a field that expects an Admin `users.id`, causing broken reads/relationships.

### E. Wrong Academic Session

Staff action writes to active session while Principal/Staff selected another session incorrectly.

### F. Cross-school possibility

Staff can write/read another school's data.

### G. Incorrect attribution

Staff action is stored/audited as though Principal performed it.
