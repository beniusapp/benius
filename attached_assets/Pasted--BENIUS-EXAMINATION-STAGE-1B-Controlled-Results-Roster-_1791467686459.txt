# BENIUS EXAMINATION — STAGE 1B
## Controlled Results Roster, Incomplete Results and Promotion Execution Protection

The Stage 1A read-only impact audit is complete.

Implement only the approved Stage 1B corrections.

BENIUS is a multi-tenant school MIS in DEVELOPMENT.

### APPROVED BUSINESS RULE

When a Student has **no applicable examination marks for the selected term**, the Student's result must be:

**Incomplete / Pending Result**

Such a Student must NOT be automatically promoted or retained.

A missing result is not equivalent to zero marks, an Absent result, or a failed examination.

### STRICT RULES

1. Keep Mobile FROZEN. Do not modify Mobile files or Mobile-specific APIs.
2. Preserve strict authenticated School ID and selected Academic Session ID isolation.
3. Archived Academic Sessions remain read-only.
4. Do not rewrite, delete, unlock, or overwrite existing locked Teacher ledger decisions.
5. Do not change existing marks, scores, attendance records, Student statuses, or Enrollment statuses.
6. Do not change configured examination policies, grade thresholds, weights, or subject-failure rules.
7. Do not alter the current partial-marks, zero-marks, or explicit-Absent calculation semantics.
8. Do not change shared Attendance-history roster behavior.
9. Do not weaken existing Promotion Stage 3 target-session Enrollment validation or atomic Registry synchronization.
10. No Production access or deployment.
11. No unrelated refactoring.
12. Stop and report before making changes if a necessary business rule or safe migration cannot be established.

# STEP 2 — DERIVE AN EXPLICIT INCOMPLETE RESULT

Update the shared examination calculation contract minimally.

For the selected school, session, class, section and term, determine whether at least one applicable configured assessment score exists.

If NO applicable marks exist:

- Overall result status = Incomplete.
- Weighted average = null.
- Grade = null / not evaluated.
- Subject failures = not evaluated, not zero.
- Promotion suggestion = Pending / no decision.
- No automatic promotion.
- No automatic retention.

Do not represent Pending as a numeric zero or as a boolean promoted/retained decision.

Preserve existing calculations for:

- Legitimate zero scores.
- Explicit Absent score records.
- Partially scored subjects or components.
- Fully evaluated results.

Use the correct term configuration; a mark from an unrelated term must not make this term complete.

If existing consumers require a boolean promoted field, maintain backward compatibility without allowing an incomplete result to be interpreted as promoted or retained. Audit every consumer and reject unsafe assumptions.

Do not expose unpublished Teacher result calculations through Student Portal endpoints.

# STEP 5 — PROTECT ADMIN PROMOTION EXECUTION

This is a critical server-side requirement.

The Stage 1A audit identified that the Admin promotion API can accept client-supplied totals and percentages without independently requiring a completed result and matching locked Teacher decision.

Implement an authoritative guard inside `executePromotionTransaction`, BEFORE any target Enrollment, promotion history, ledger or Registry-related writes.

The guard must:

1. Resolve the authenticated school and exact source Academic Session.
2. Validate the Student's exact active source-session Enrollment and source class/section.
3. Identify the applicable examination term and its configured components.
4. Re-evaluate result completeness from persisted, correctly scoped examination records.
5. Reject no-mark or incomplete results under the approved rule.
6. Require a matching valid Teacher promotion decision for the same school, session, Student, source class/section and term.
7. Require the decision to be locked and eligible for execution.
8. Reject conflicting, missing, stale or invalid decisions.
9. Never trust client-supplied percentages, grade, promotion eligibility or decision status as authority.
10. Preserve the existing target-session checks and Promotion Stage 3 protections.

If the existing Admin wizard uses a different term identifier from raw exam-score types, resolve the mapping through authoritative term configuration. Do not guess, silently fall back, or treat an empty score aggregation as complete.

If safe mapping or stale-decision verification cannot be established with existing data, fail closed and report the blocker. Do not invent a permissive fallback.

Ensure the complete validation and subsequent writes remain atomic and safe against concurrent score, ledger or promotion changes. If the current transaction boundaries cannot guarantee this, report the required locking/versioning design before implementing a partial guard.

For the existing three conflicting locked decisions:

- Preserve the rows.
- Do not execute them.
- Do not change their stored status.
- Surface the conflict to Admin where feasible.

Do not silently perform a bulk repair.
