Keep the modal title as:

Record Offline Payment

Bank Transfer | Demand Draft

- Online

- Any other payment method

4. Method-Specific Fields

Immediately below the payment-method selector, show only the fields relevant to the selected method.

Cash

Show:

- Received Date
- Cash denomination grid
- Cash total
- Payment verification

The denomination calculation and server-side validation already implemented must remain unchanged.

Do not add an unnecessary manually editable payment amount.

---

Cheque

Show:

- Received Date
- Cheque Number — required
- Cheque Date — required
- Bank Name
- Branch — optional
- Payer Name — optional
- Notes — optional

The payment amount must come from the invoice outstanding amount.

Do not require the admin to manually enter the cheque amount if the invoice already determines the exact payment amount.

Cheque should be treated as:

Paid / Completed immediately after successful recording.

Do not introduce any pending-clearance state.

---

Bank Transfer

Show:

- Received Date
- UTR / Transaction Reference — required
- Transfer Date — required
- Sender's Bank
- Branch — optional
- Sender Name — optional
- Notes — optional

Again, the payment amount comes directly from the invoice outstanding amount.

---

Demand Draft

Show:

- Received Date
- DD Number — required
- DD Date — required
- Issuing Bank
- Issuing Branch
- Payer Name — optional
- Notes — optional

The DD amount must come directly from the invoice outstanding amount.

Treat the DD as:

Paid / Completed immediately after successful recording.

Do not introduce a pending-clearance state.
