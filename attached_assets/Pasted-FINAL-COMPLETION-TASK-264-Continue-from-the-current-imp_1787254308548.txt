8. Resolution source is persisted.

E. Unresolved webhook retention test

Create a validly signed refund/dispute webhook where:

- payment ID is unknown;
- order ID is unknown;
- refund/dispute cannot be associated with a stored payment;
- notes do not safely establish a tenant.

Expected:

The system must retain the event/delivery where safe, but explicitly mark it unresolved.

For example:

school_id = NULL
fee_record_id = NULL

Do not guess the tenant.

Do not attach it to an invoice from another school.

Do not silently discard the signed webhook.

F. Cross-tenant isolation test

Create payment/order identities belonging to School A.

Then attempt resolution using misleading notes or identifiers associated with School B.

Expected:

- School A identity remains authoritative.
- School B must never receive the event.
- If safe resolution is impossible, retain it as unresolved.
- No cross-tenant payment attempt or invoice association occurs.

Explicitly test:

- payment ID;
- order ID;
- refund ID;
- dispute ID.
