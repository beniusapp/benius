## PART A — DATABASE-BACKED REGRESSION TESTS

Test the complete Teacher Homework review lifecycle:

1. Student submits a text-only Homework answer.
2. Authorized assigned Teacher can read that submission.
3. Teacher approves with a comment.
4. Student sees the approval and comment.
5. Approved submission cannot be edited or resubmitted.
6. Teacher requests resubmission on a separate submitted answer.
7. Student sees the request and comment.
8. Student resubmits successfully.
9. Previous review metadata does not incorrectly describe the new submission.
10. Teacher can review the resubmitted answer again.
11. Review actions work without requiring a comment.
12. Submissions containing attachments remain blocked from Teacher review under the current deferred-storage policy.

## PART B — SECURITY TESTS

Verify:

- Teacher cannot access another school's submissions.
- Teacher cannot review outside the established assigned class/section authorization.
- Student cannot read another Student's private answer or Teacher feedback.
- Cross-session submission access is rejected.
- Archived-session review writes are rejected.
- Student resubmission in archived sessions is rejected.
- Client-supplied school or reviewer IDs cannot bypass server-side authorization.
- Teacher review API does not expose attachment URLs.

## PART C — VIEW TRACKING

Verify:

- Student opening Homework records a view.
- Merely loading the Homework list does not record a view.
- Reopening does not increase the count.
- Concurrent opens cannot create duplicate view records.
- Active roster counts only eligible active Students with active selected-session Enrollments.
- Archived-session counts follow historical roster rules.
- View tracking does not modify academic content in archived sessions.
- Existing Homework red-dot notifications are unaffected.
