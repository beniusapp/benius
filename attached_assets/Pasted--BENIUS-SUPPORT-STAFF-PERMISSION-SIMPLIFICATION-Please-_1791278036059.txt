# MEANING OF "FULL ACCESS LIKE PRINCIPAL"

For these modules:

> When the parent module is granted, Support Staff should be able to perform the normal operational functionality that Principal/Admin can perform inside that module.

However, this does NOT mean bypassing:

- tenant isolation
- Academic Session isolation
- historical-session read-only rules
- server-side validation
- normal data integrity/business rules

Support Staff is still a Support Staff actor operating on the Principal's shared school data.

Do NOT convert Support Staff into an Admin identity.
