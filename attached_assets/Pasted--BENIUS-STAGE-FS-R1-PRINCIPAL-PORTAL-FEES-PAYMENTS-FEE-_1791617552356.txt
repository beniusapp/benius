## 1. Confirmed product architecture

The following decisions are FINAL for this audit:

1. Fee Structure definitions are **SCHOOL-GLOBAL**.
2. Fee Structures are shared across Academic Sessions within the same school.
3. Generated student fee invoices are **ACADEMIC-SESSION-SPECIFIC**.
4. Invoice operations must be scoped to the authenticated school and appropriate Academic Session.
5. Archived-session invoices must retain their historical financial values.
6. Fee Structure changes must not silently rewrite paid invoices or archived financial history.
7. Application-facing dates and times use **Asia/Kolkata (IST)**.
8. Mobile application and Mobile APIs are completely OUT OF SCOPE.

Do not propose converting Fee Structures into session-specific definitions unless a concrete, serious architectural conflict is discovered. Even then, report it only; do not implement it.

## 4. Verify multi-tenant isolation

This is a critical BENIUS requirement.

Inspect all Fee Structure read and write routes.

Verify:

1. School ID comes from the authenticated session.
2. School A cannot read School B's Fee Structures.
3. School A cannot create, edit or delete School B's Fee Structures.
4. A structure ID belonging to another school cannot bypass ownership checks.
5. Related class/category references cannot cross school boundaries.
6. Fee Structure synchronization cannot affect another school's invoices.
7. Error responses do not expose another school's private data.

Report any confirmed weakness with exact file and line references.

Do not attempt unauthorized access against real schools.

## 5. Verify Academic Session behavior

Fee Structures are intentionally global.

However, generated invoices are session-specific.

Inspect how the application handles:

- The selected Academic Session.
- The school's active Academic Session.
- Archived Academic Sessions.
- Creating or editing global Fee Structures while an archived session is selected.
- Generating invoices.
- Synchronizing existing invoices.
- Removing invoices after a structure's applicable classes change.
- Late-fee configuration changes.

Determine whether invoice-affecting operations are restricted to the authenticated school's ACTIVE session where intended.

Also verify that creating a new Academic Session does not automatically copy old session-owned invoices into the new session.

Do not change existing behavior.
