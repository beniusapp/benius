# CONFIRMED ROOT CAUSE

BENIUS business/display timezone is:

**Asia/Kolkata / IST**

Teacher Homework/Classwork forms already display today's correct IST date.

However:

- Homework/Classwork do not store a separate business-date column.
- `created_at` represents the creation instant using the project's UTC convention.
- Student Homework/Classwork filtering currently uses raw `created_at::date`.
- Student cards derive their displayed work date from the raw UTC date portion.

Therefore work created shortly after midnight IST can appear under the previous date.

Example:

```text
Created:
07 Oct 2026 01:08 IST

Stored UTC equivalent:
06 Oct 2026 19:38

Current Student business-date interpretation:
06 Oct ❌

Correct BENIUS business date:
07 Oct ✅
```

Fix this consistently.
