Task: Audit and Correct Payment Method — “Online” vs “Portal Payment”

I noticed an important consistency issue in the Fees & Payments module.

When a fee is paid by a student through the Student Portal, the payment method is currently being displayed as:

«Online»

But for our application, a payment initiated through the Student Portal should be displayed consistently as:

«Portal Payment»

This appears to be happening in multiple places, including the Ledger and Transaction Report. I do NOT want a superficial UI-only text replacement.

1. Investigate the root cause first

Before changing anything, trace the complete payment-method data flow:

Student Portal payment → Razorpay/payment creation → payment records/payment attempts → webhook processing → database → payment-method normalization → Ledger → Transaction Report → Export Ledger → receipts/payment details

Find exactly where ""Online"" is being assigned, transformed, normalized, or displayed.

Determine whether:

- ""Online"" is actually stored in the database
- ""Online"" is generated by backend normalization
- ""Online"" is derived from Razorpay's payment method
- ""Online"" is a frontend display fallback
- Different payment sources are being incorrectly mapped to the same value

Do not assume the cause.

2. Important business rule

A payment's origin/channel and its underlying payment instrument are not necessarily the same thing.

For example:

A student pays ₹12,000 from the Student Portal using Razorpay.

The payment may technically use a Razorpay payment instrument such as UPI, card, netbanking, etc.

But in our Fees & Payments business UI, when the payment was initiated through the Student Portal, the high-level payment method/channel should be:

Portal Payment

Do NOT destroy useful underlying Razorpay/payment-instrument information if it is required elsewhere.

If the system already stores both payment origin/channel and the actual Razorpay method, preserve both where appropriate.

3. Required display behavior

Use this mapping consistently wherever the application displays the payment method:

Payment source / method| Display
Student Portal payment| Portal Payment
Cash| Cash
UPI / QR| UPI / QR
Bank Transfer| Bank Transfer
Demand Draft| Demand Draft
Cheque| Cheque
Other legitimate offline methods| Their actual configured method

The key requirement is:

«Student Portal-originated payments must NOT be displayed as “Online” in the business-facing payment-method field. They must display as “Portal Payment”.»

Do not change the database schema unless it is genuinely necessary.

6. Preserve actual payment information

Do not break or remove Razorpay information.

The application should preserve the underlying payment information wherever it is useful for:

Only the appropriate business-facing payment-method display should be normalized to Portal Payment.
