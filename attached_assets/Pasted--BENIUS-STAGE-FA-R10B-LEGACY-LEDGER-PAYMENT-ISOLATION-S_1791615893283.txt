`payment_records.session_id` is nullable.

The Ledger payment-list query appears to filter directly by `payment_records.session_id`.

Financial Analytics may instead attribute payments through their linked invoice's Academic Session.

Investigate this carefully.

Trace:

payment_records
→ fee_record_id
→ fee_records
→ invoice.session_id
→ authenticated school
→ selected Academic Session
→ Ledger display
→ Financial Analytics
→ Ledger PDF/CSV exports
→ Payment receipt and transaction detail.

Identify all relevant queries and exact source locations.

Do not assume all Ledger paths behave identically.

**Existing Session Events**

Displays events explicitly associated with the selected Academic Session.

**Proposed School-Wide Events**
