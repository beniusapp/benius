# BENIUS — REMOVE SCHOOL SETUP FROM SUPPORT STAFF

Please make this small focused correction.

## Product Decision

**School Setup is Principal/Admin-only.**

Support Staff must NOT be able to access or manage School Setup, even if an old Support Staff account still has legacy School Setup permission values stored.

### Remove School Setup completely from the Support Staff permission editor.

This includes all its submodules:

- Academic Sessions
- Classes
- Sections
- Subjects
- Exam Types
- Class–Section Mapping
- Class–Subject Mapping
- Class–Exam Type Mapping
- Academic Policy (Grading)
- Exam & Promotion Policy
- Leave Policy
- Attendance Policy

## Required behavior

For Support Staff:

- School Setup must NOT appear in **Edit Permissions**.
- School Setup must NOT appear on the Support Staff dashboard.
- Support Staff must NOT access School Setup by manually entering a URL.
- Support Staff must NOT use School Setup management APIs.
- Existing/legacy `school-setup` permission strings must NOT give access.

For Principal/Admin:

- School Setup remains completely unchanged and fully functional.

## IMPORTANT — Academic Session Selector

Do **NOT** remove or break the Support Staff Academic Session selector.

Support Staff may still see/select the school's Academic Session for modules they are authorized to use.

That is separate from **Academic Session management**, which remains Principal/Admin-only.

## Scope

Do NOT change other Support Staff permissions.

Do NOT change Fees & Payments.

Do NOT change the Academic Session selector/foundation.

Do NOT change Mobile.

Do NOT change database schema.

Do NOT deploy.

## Verify

Confirm:

- Support Staff permission editor → School Setup absent
- Support Staff dashboard → School Setup absent
- Direct URL → blocked
- School Setup APIs → blocked for Support Staff
- Legacy stored School Setup permission → still blocked
- Principal/Admin → unchanged
- Support Staff Academic Session selector → still works

Return files changed, tests/builds performed, and:
