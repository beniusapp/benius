# BENIUS — FEES & PAYMENTS
## SUPPORT STAFF — BULK INVOICE GENERATION CORRECTION

This is a SMALL follow-up to the completed Fees & Payments Support Staff permission work.

Do NOT modify any other module.

Do NOT deploy.

---

## PRODUCT DECISION

**Bulk Invoice Generation is NOT Principal/Admin-only.**

If a Support Staff member has the appropriate granted Fees & Payments area permission, that Support Staff member must be able to perform the existing **Bulk Invoice Generation** operation in the same normal way as Principal/Admin.

---

## FIRST — LOCATE THE EXISTING FUNCTION

Before changing authorization, identify exactly:

- where Bulk Invoice Generation exists in the current Fees & Payments Web UI;
- which Fees tab/functional area owns it;
- which frontend action/button triggers it;
- which API endpoint performs it;
- which backend authorization currently makes it Admin-only.

Do NOT create a new Bulk Invoice Generation tab.

Do NOT create a new Support Staff permission merely because of this correction.

Use the existing Fees functional-area permission that actually owns this operation.

For example, if the existing implementation proves that Bulk Invoice Generation belongs to **Ledger & Transactions**, then:

> `Ledger & Transactions` granted → Bulk Invoice Generation allowed.

Do not assume this example; verify the actual implementation.

---

## REQUIRED BEHAVIOR

### Principal/Admin

Existing Bulk Invoice Generation behavior remains unchanged.

### Support Staff WITH the owning Fees permission

Bulk Invoice Generation:

**ALLOW**

Support Staff should be able to perform the existing operation normally.

### Support Staff WITHOUT the owning Fees permission

Bulk Invoice Generation:

**REJECT**

Both UI and backend must enforce this.

---

## SECURITY

Do NOT simply remove the Admin guard.

Authorize Support Staff only when:

- authenticated as Support Staff;
- Support Staff belongs to the same school/tenant;
- required Fees & Payments functional-area permission is granted;
- requested data/resources belong to that school;
- existing Academic Session rules are satisfied.

School A Support Staff must NEVER generate invoices for School B.

Previous/inactive Academic Sessions must remain read-only wherever the existing invoice operation is session-specific.

---

## EXTERNAL PORTAL

NO CHANGE.

External Portal remains:

**PRINCIPAL/ADMIN ONLY.**

Giving Support Staff Bulk Invoice Generation access must NOT provide External Portal access.

---

## DO NOT CHANGE YET

Do NOT change:

- Refund authorization
- Single-record deletion authorization
- Bulk deletion authorization

Those are separate product decisions.

Only correct **Bulk Invoice Generation** in this step.

## PERMISSION UI

Keep the existing five Support Staff Fees permissions:

- Financial Analytics
- Fee Structures
- Ledger & Transactions
- Reminders
- Audit Log

Do NOT add:

- Bulk Invoice Generation

as a sixth permission.

It should inherit the permission of the Fees functional area where the existing operation actually belongs.

External Portal must remain absent from Support Staff permissions.
