The purpose of this step is to establish the exact current behavior of Attendance calculations when a Student changes class/section during the SAME Academic Session.

---

# CONTEXT

Previous Attendance audits established:

* Student Registry is GLOBAL/PERMANENT.
* Academic Session enrollment is separate from the global Student Registry.
* Attendance is school + Academic Session scoped.
* Historical Attendance preserves class/section context.
* Direct Teacher marking is server-authoritative to the active Academic Session.
* Leave approval now obeys active Session, archived Session, 7-day, holiday, and class/section integrity rules.
* Same-school Teachers do NOT require Faculty Mapping to mark Attendance.
* Sunday stored-mark behavior is already accepted.
* Historical deleted-Student identity preservation is implemented in development.

The latest audit identified one remaining calculation concern:

> A same-Session class transfer can hide earlier Attendance marks from Student yearly/statistics calculations.

The current observation was:

* Monthly may read all Attendance records for the Student.
* Yearly/statistics may resolve one Session enrollment class/section and filter Attendance to that class/section.
* Admin daily aggregation may retain the historical class/section saved on the Attendance record.

This MUST be verified against the actual current code.

---

# OBJECTIVE

Determine exactly whether a Student who changes class/section during the SAME Academic Session can have earlier Attendance excluded from:

* yearly totals
* statistics
* other cumulative Attendance calculations

Also determine whether the behavior is intentional or an implementation inconsistency.

Do NOT decide the product policy.

Do NOT propose a fix yet.

At the end, classify the observed behavior into one of these categories:

### E — MIXED
