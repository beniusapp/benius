These are lifecycle events associated with the same payment attempt.

"payment_attempts"

"payment_webhook_events" or equivalent event table

"fee_audit_log"

Remains the broader application audit trail.

"payment_records"

Represents actual collected payment/receipt information.

Do not force all of these concepts into one table.
