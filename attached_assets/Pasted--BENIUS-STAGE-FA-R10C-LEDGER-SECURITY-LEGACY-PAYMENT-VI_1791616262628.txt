# BENIUS — STAGE FA-R10C
## LEDGER SECURITY, LEGACY PAYMENT VISIBILITY & ACADEMIC SESSION ISOLATION VERIFICATION

### STRICT READ-ONLY — SYNTHETIC TESTING ONLY — NO FIXES AUTHORIZED

BENIUS is a multi-tenant School Management Information System currently in DEVELOPMENT.

FA-R10B identified three source-level issues requiring further verification:

1. A payment-list invoice join may expose another school's invoice number if payment and invoice school ownership disagree.
2. A legacy payment with NULL `payment_records.session_id` may be omitted from an archived-session Ledger payment list even though its linked invoice identifies the correct session.
3. A payment with a non-NULL session ID different from its invoice session may be attributed differently across Ledger, Analytics, and exports.

The owner now authorizes a focused, non-destructive verification of these issues.

**This stage does NOT authorize implementation.**

## 3. Independently test six mandatory scenarios

Create synthetic data for two schools and multiple Academic Sessions.

### Scenario A — Normal archived payment

School A:
- Invoice belongs to Archived Session 2026–2027.
- Payment belongs to School A.
- Payment session is 2026–2027.
- Payment links to the correct invoice.

Expected:

Payment appears in the 2026–2027 Ledger payment list.

It must not appear in another school's Ledger.

### Scenario B — Legacy NULL-session payment

School A:
- Invoice belongs to Archived Session 2026–2027.
- Payment belongs to School A.
- Payment `session_id = NULL`.
- Payment links to the correct invoice and student.

Verify whether the payment appears in:

- Ledger payment list.
- Ledger summary.
- Financial Analytics.
- Invoice detail.
- CSV export.
- PDF export.
- Payment transaction report.

Expected business ownership:

The invoice belongs to 2026–2027, so the linked payment's financial ownership is associated with that invoice session.

Do not modify the payment row.

### Scenario C — Conflicting session IDs

School A:
- Invoice belongs to Archived Session 2026–2027.
- Payment has `session_id = 2027–2028`.

Verify:

- Which session's Ledger list contains the payment.
- Which session's summary contains it.
- Which session's Analytics contains it.
- Which session's exports contain it.
- Whether a mismatch warning is available.

Do not silently repair or normalize the record.

Clearly distinguish observed current behavior from the recommended future policy.

### Scenario D — Cross-school invoice link

School A payment:
- `payment_records.school_id = School A`.
- Linked invoice belongs to School B.

Verify whether the School A payment-list query can return:

- School B invoice number.
- School B student information.
- School B invoice amount.
- Any other School B metadata.

Check whether the invoice join enforces both:

- Matching invoice ID.
- Matching school ownership.

This is a security-sensitive scenario.

**No cross-school invoice metadata should be exposed under any circumstances.**

Do not use real school data.

### Scenario E — Orphan payment

School A payment:
- Valid School A ownership.
- No valid linked invoice.

Verify how the payment is handled in:

- Session-filtered payment lists.
- Summaries.
- Analytics.
- Receipts.
- Exports.

Do not invent an invoice session for an orphan payment.

### Scenario F — Processed refund on a legacy payment

School A:
- Archived-session invoice.
- Linked payment with NULL session ID.
- Processed refund associated with that invoice/payment.

Verify:

- Ledger payment-list inclusion.
- Financial Analytics outstanding.
- Ledger summary outstanding.
- Ledger CSV/PDF outstanding.
- Transaction report refund attribution.

Do not change refund formulas.

Report any disagreement separately.
