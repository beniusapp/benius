Objective

Implement a professional Invoice PDF + Payment Receipt PDF system in the Fees/Ledger module.

The goal is to clearly distinguish an Invoice from a Payment Receipt, provide downloadable PDFs at the correct stage of the payment lifecycle, and maintain complete auditability.

1. Invoice PDF

Every newly created invoice must have a professionally designed PDF available while the invoice is Due / Overdue.

The invoice PDF should be:

- Modern
- Premium and professional
- Clean and easy to read
- A4-friendly
- Suitable for printing and digital sharing
- Consistent with the school's branding
- Clearly identified as INVOICE

The Invoice should prominently display:

INVOICE

and a highly visible:

Invoice No. INV-XXXX

Include all relevant invoice information already captured by the system, including:

School Details

- School logo
- School name
- School address
- Phone
- Official email
- Affiliation number
- GSTIN, if available

Student / Parent Details

- Student name
- Student ID / MIS ID
- Parent/Guardian name — use the actual parent/guardian field already available in the system
- Class
- Section

Invoice Information

- Invoice Number
- Invoice Date & Time
- Academic Session
- Frequency
- Fee Period
- Due Date

2. Exact Invoice Date & Time

The invoice must display the actual persisted invoice creation timestamp.

Example:

Invoice Date & Time: 19 Aug 2026, 09:08:34 PM IST

Rules:

- Use the server-side persisted creation timestamp.
- Display it in "Asia/Kolkata" / IST.
- Include date, hours, minutes, seconds and "IST".
- Never use the browser/device time.
- Never generate a new timestamp when opening, printing or downloading the invoice.
- The timestamp must remain unchanged permanently for that invoice.

This must work identically for both:

- Add Invoice
- Generate Invoices

3. Invoice Line Details

Clearly display:

- Fee Name / Description
- Fee Type
- Frequency
- Fee Period
- Amount

If the invoice contains fee components, display a professional Fee Breakdown / Components section containing:

- Component
- Description
- Amount
- Component subtotal

The component subtotal must remain consistent with the invoice amount according to the existing validation rules.

4. Amount Presentation

Show the amount prominently.

For example:

TOTAL PAYABLE ₹1,000

Also display the amount in words:

Amount in Words: One Thousand Rupees Only

The amount in words must be generated automatically from the actual invoice amount and must never be manually entered.

5. Late Fee & Penalty

If enabled, clearly display:

- Late Fee & Penalty: Enabled
- Rule type
- Penalty amount/rate
- Relevant configuration already stored on the invoice

If disabled:

Late Fee & Penalty: Disabled

Do not invent or recalculate historical configuration when generating the PDF. Use the invoice's saved configuration/snapshot.

---

6. Invoice vs Receipt — VERY IMPORTANT

The Invoice and Payment Receipt must have different visual identities so users can immediately understand the difference.

Invoice Design

Use a professional navy/blue-based visual identity.

It should communicate:

Amount Due / Payment Required

Use the school's branding together with tasteful navy/blue accents.

The document must prominently say:

INVOICE

and show:

STATUS: DUE

or:

STATUS: OVERDUE

when applicable.

Do not make the design excessively colorful. It should remain professional and look good both digitally and when printed.

Payment Receipt Design

Use a professional green/teal-based visual identity.

It should communicate:

Payment Successfully Received

The document must prominently say:

PAYMENT RECEIPT

and clearly show:

Receipt No.

The receipt should also show the relevant invoice number so the payment can be traced back to the original invoice.

For example:

Invoice No.: INV-0094
Receipt No.: ON-0007

The receipt should have a clear:

PAID / PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL

visual status.

---

7. Invoice Download / Print Lifecycle

Add an Invoice PDF action to the Ledger/Transactions table.

For an invoice that is:

Due

Show:

📄 Download Invoice

and, if the existing UI supports printing:

🖨️ Print Invoice

Overdue

The Invoice PDF remains available.

Show:

📄 Download Invoice

and optionally:

🖨️ Print Invoice

Paid

Once the invoice becomes Paid:

The normal Invoice download/print action must no longer be available from the active invoice actions.

Instead, show:

🧾 Download Receipt

and/or:

🖨️ Print Receipt

The user should be guided toward the payment receipt because the invoice has already been settled.

---

8. IMPORTANT — Do Not Delete the Invoice

When an invoice becomes Paid:

DO NOT delete the invoice.

DO NOT modify its historical invoice information.

DO NOT invalidate or overwrite the invoice number.

The invoice must remain permanently stored for:

- Audit
- Accounting
- Historical records
- Reporting
- Payment reconciliation
- Academic-session history

Only the user-facing download/print action changes after payment.

The system cannot prevent someone from retaining a PDF that they downloaded before payment. The requirement is only to prevent new invoice downloads/prints through the application after payment.

---

9. Receipt Availability

After successful payment:

The system should provide the Payment Receipt PDF.

The receipt must use the existing payment/receipt data and include, where available:

- School details
- Student name
- Parent/Guardian name
- Student ID / MIS ID
- Class
- Section
- Invoice number
- Receipt number
- Payment date & time
- Payment method
- Amount paid
- Amount in words
- Payment ID
- Razorpay Order ID, if applicable
- Razorpay Payment ID, if applicable
- Auth Code, if available
- Fee name
- Fee type
- Fee period
- Academic session
- Components/breakdown
- Any applicable concession/late fee information
- Payment status

The receipt must clearly communicate that payment has been successfully received.

---

10. Ledger Action Logic

The Actions column should automatically reflect the invoice/payment state.

Due / Overdue

Show the Invoice action:

📄 Invoice

Paid

Replace the Invoice action with:

🧾 Receipt

Do not show both as active download actions in the normal ledger view.

However, do not delete or alter the underlying invoice record.

---

11. PDF Generation

Do not simply print the browser page as the PDF.

Generate a proper professional A4 invoice/receipt document using the application's PDF generation system.

The PDF must:

- Have proper A4 dimensions
- Have consistent margins
- Have professional typography
- Have clear section hierarchy
- Avoid excessive empty space
- Avoid tiny unreadable text
- Avoid clipped content
- Automatically handle longer fee names/descriptions
- Automatically handle multiple components
- Remain readable when printed
- Stay within a clean page layout
- Use the school's logo when available

If content exceeds one page, handle pagination professionally rather than shrinking everything to an unreadable size.

---

12. Data Integrity

Do not create a separate competing invoice/payment data model.

Use the existing canonical:

- "fee_records"
- invoice number
- payment records
- payment attempts
- receipt number
- Razorpay payment/order data
- existing snapshots

The PDF must be generated from the authoritative stored invoice/payment data.

Do not use client-submitted values as the authoritative source for historical invoice/receipt information.

---

13. Add Invoice and Generate Invoices

This feature must work identically for invoices created through:

Add Invoice

and:

Generate Invoices

Both must produce the same professional Invoice PDF structure.

The only difference is how the invoice data was created.

Downstream behavior must remain unchanged for:

- Payments
- Razorpay
- Webhooks
- Payment attempts
- Receipts
- Ledger
- Transactions
- Late fees
- Reporting
- Duplicate protection
- Academic sessions
- Tenant isolation

---

14. Existing Invoice Number Must Remain Permanent

The Invoice Number must never be replaced by a Receipt Number.

Example:

Invoice creation:

INV-0094

After payment:

Invoice No.: INV-0094
Receipt No.: ON-0007

Never overwrite "invoice_number" with the receipt number.

---

15. Exact Payment Timestamp

For the receipt, use the actual persisted successful payment timestamp.

Display it in:

Asia/Kolkata / IST

Example:

Payment Date & Time: 19 Aug 2026, 09:15:42 PM IST

Do not use browser/device time.

Do not generate a new timestamp when the receipt is viewed or downloaded.

---

16. Security and Authorization

Before allowing Invoice or Receipt PDF access:

- Verify the authenticated user's school/tenant.
- Verify the invoice belongs to that school.
- Verify the user has the appropriate permission.
- Do not expose another school's invoice/receipt.
- Do not allow invoice IDs from another tenant to retrieve documents.

Maintain the existing tenant-isolation and authorization rules.

---

17. Final UI Goal

The final user experience should be extremely clear:

Invoice lifecycle

Invoice Created
↓
📄 Download Invoice
↓
Payment Made
↓
Invoice becomes Paid
↓
🧾 Download Payment Receipt

The Invoice should visually communicate:

“This is what you need to pay.”

The Receipt should visually communicate:

“This is proof that you have paid.”

Both documents should look like professionally designed documents from a modern premium school ERP — not like a browser printout or basic table.

Before making changes, inspect the existing invoice PDF and receipt generation code and reuse existing infrastructure where possible. Do not break existing payment, Razorpay, receipt, invoice-number, or audit functionality.

After implementation, test:

1. Add Invoice → Due → Invoice PDF
2. Generate Invoice → Due → Invoice PDF
3. Due → Paid → Invoice action replaced by Receipt action
4. Paid → Receipt PDF
5. Invoice number remains unchanged after payment
6. Receipt number is generated only through the existing successful-payment flow
7. Exact invoice creation timestamp displays correctly in IST
8. Exact successful payment timestamp displays correctly in IST
9. Amount in words is correct
10. Parent/Guardian name is taken from the existing system field
11. Multiple fee components render correctly
12. Long fee names/components do not break the PDF layout
13. Tenant/security isolation remains intact
14. Existing Razorpay and payment flows continue to work
15. Both Add Invoice and Generate Invoices produce the same professional invoice format

Do not change unrelated Fees, Payments, Razorpay, or Ledger behavior.
