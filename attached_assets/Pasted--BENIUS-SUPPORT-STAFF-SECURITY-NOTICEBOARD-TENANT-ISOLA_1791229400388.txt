# CONFIRMED PROBLEM

An authenticated Support Staff member belongs to one specific school.

Example:

```text
Support Staff X
→ School A
```

However, the current Web Noticeboard routes can accept a client-supplied `schoolId` without consistently validating it against the authenticated Support Staff's school.

The audit specifically identified:

- `GET /api/notices/:schoolId`
- `GET /api/notices/:schoolId/all`
- `POST /api/notices`

For Support Staff, the negative compatibility `userId` can satisfy older truthy authentication checks.

Therefore a School A Support Staff session may potentially request or create Noticeboard data against School B.

This violates BENIUS's permanent multi-tenant rule.

---

# NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT RULE

A Support Staff account belongs to ONE school.

Therefore:

```text
Support Staff → School A

School A Noticeboard
→ potentially allowed according to authorization/permissions

School B Noticeboard
→ ALWAYS REJECT
```

Support Staff must NEVER:

- read School B notices;
- create a notice for School B;
- edit School B notices;
- delete School B notices;
- use School B students/classes/sections as Notice targets;
- obtain School B Noticeboard data through manipulated URL/body/query values.

---

For Support Staff:

Do NOT trust:

# READ ROUTES

Correct:

`GET /api/notices/:schoolId`

and

`GET /api/notices/:schoolId/all`

For Support Staff:

1. Authenticate the Support Staff session.
2. Resolve their actual school from the authenticated server identity.
3. Compare the requested `:schoolId` against the authenticated school.
4. Same school → continue with existing Noticeboard behavior.
5. Different school → REJECT before querying/returning Notice data.

Do not return School B data.

Use the project's established safe error behavior.

---

# CREATE ROUTE

Correct:

For Support Staff:
