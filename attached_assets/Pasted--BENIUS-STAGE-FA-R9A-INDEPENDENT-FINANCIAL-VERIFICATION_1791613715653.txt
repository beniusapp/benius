# PART B — THREE APPROVED CORRECTIONS ONLY

## 8. Correction 1 — Reject fractional cash quantities

File:

`artifacts/api-server/src/financial-analytics-data.ts`

Current confirmed defect:

A cash denomination quantity such as `1.9` is silently truncated to `1`.

Required correction:

- Accept only finite positive integer quantities.
- Reject fractional quantities rather than truncating them.
- Reject zero, negative, NaN, and non-finite quantities.
- Preserve valid existing integer quantities.
- Preserve the existing handling of malformed breakdowns and undocumented cash coverage, except for the approved invalid-quantity rejection.
- Do not change recorded cash payment amounts.
- Do not change Gross Collected.
- Do not change cash payment processing or stored denomination records.

Test both valid and invalid quantities.

## 9. Correction 2 — Reject leading-zero denomination keys

File:

`artifacts/api-server/src/financial-analytics-data.ts`

Current confirmed defect:

A denomination key such as `"0500"` is accepted and normalized to `500`.

Required correction:

- Accept only canonical positive-integer denomination keys.
- Reject `"0500"`, `"00"`, `"0"`, negative values, and malformed keys.
- Continue accepting valid keys such as `"500"`, `"200"`, `"100"`, `"50"`, and other positive integer denominations already accepted by the application.
- Do not introduce a new denomination allow-list.
- Do not change recorded payment amounts.
- Do not modify existing denomination data.
- Do not change financial formulas.

Use strict validation before numeric conversion.

## 10. Correction 3 — Correct Class Performance PDF heading

`artifacts/api-server/src/financial-analytics-pdf.ts`
