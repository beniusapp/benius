3. STUDENT IDENTITY & TENANT ISOLATION
==================================================

This application is a TRUE MULTI-TENANT SaaS platform.

School/tenant isolation is mandatory.

Determine exactly how a Student belongs to a school.

Verify whether Student identity is based on:

- studentId
- DSID
- userId
- schoolId
- tenantId
- email
- phone
- another identifier

Determine whether the same DSID/email/phone can exist in multiple schools.

Identify every student lookup used by authentication or recovery.

For every lookup, determine whether schoolId is enforced.

CRITICAL:

A Student from School A must NEVER be able to:

- discover whether a School B student exists
- trigger recovery for a School B student
- receive or validate a School B OTP
- reset a School B student's password
- use a School B student's recovery token
- affect a School B student's sessions

Do not assume tenant isolation from frontend behavior.

Inspect the actual backend/database queries.
