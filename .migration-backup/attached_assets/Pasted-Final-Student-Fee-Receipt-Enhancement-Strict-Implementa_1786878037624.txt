Final Student Fee Receipt Enhancement — Strict Implementation Specification

We are enhancing the existing Student Fee Payment Receipt in our multi-tenant school ERP.

IMPORTANT: READ THIS FIRST

Before modifying anything, thoroughly inspect the existing implementation and architecture.

Do not assume that a field exists merely because it is mentioned below.

For every field:

1. Identify its actual source table/column/API.
2. Confirm how and when the value is written.
3. Confirm whether it is immutable/historical.
4. Confirm whether it is available for both online and offline payments.
5. Use the existing canonical source of truth.
6. Do not derive historical information from current fee configuration.
7. Do not invent, guess, approximate, or fabricate values.
8. If the required value is genuinely unavailable/null, display "—".
9. Never display a misleading value merely to make the receipt look complete.

This is a receipt accuracy and auditability enhancement, not a redesign of the payment system.

Do not modify Razorpay payment processing, webhook logic, payment calculations, receipt-number generation, fee-period generation, or unrelated business logic unless absolutely required to safely expose an already-existing canonical value.

2. DATA ACCURACY RULE — ABSOLUTE REQUIREMENT

The receipt must use actual stored historical data.

Never do this:

- Do not calculate a past concession from today's fee structure.
- Do not recalculate a historical late fee.
- Do not derive the fee month from payment date.
- Do not derive the invoice date from payment date.
- Do not derive the due date from today's configuration.
- Do not use the current fee structure to reconstruct an old invoice.
- Do not assume "Tuition Fee" if the actual fee type is different.
- Do not assume a payment method.
- Do not fabricate reference numbers.
- Do not fabricate payer names.
- Do not fabricate student information.

If a value does not exist in the canonical historical record:

Display "—".

Example:

"Bank RRN: —"

is correct when the RRN is unavailable.

An incorrect value is worse than a missing value.

6. FEE PERIOD — VERY IMPORTANT

The receipt must make the fee period extremely clear.

This is NOT the same thing as the payment date.

Use the already implemented immutable:

- "fee_period_start"
- "fee_period_end"

and the existing "feePeriodLabel()" / fee-period architecture.

Examples:

Monthly:

"Fee Period: August 2026"

Quarterly:

"Fee Period: April–June 2026"

Annual:

"Academic Session: 2026–27"

Critical rule

Never derive the fee period from:

- payment date
- invoice generation date
- current date
- current fee structure

The stored invoice fee period is authoritative.

Example:

Invoice Date: 10 October 2026
Fee Period: August 2026
Due Date: 10 September 2026
Payment Date: 15 October 2026

The receipt must still say:

Fee Period: August 2026

even if the payment occurred months later.
