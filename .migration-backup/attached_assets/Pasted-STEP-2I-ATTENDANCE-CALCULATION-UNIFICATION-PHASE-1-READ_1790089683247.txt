==================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE PROJECT RULES
==================================================

1. MULTI-TENANT ISOLATION
Every Attendance calculation/read must remain strictly scoped to:
- authenticated school/tenant
- selected/authoritative Academic Session

Never calculate or combine Attendance data across schools or sessions.

2. STUDENT REGISTRY
Student Registry is global/current.
It must NOT be treated as historical Attendance membership.

3. ENROLLMENT
Enrollment is NOT an Attendance prerequisite.
Do not introduce any Enrollment gate for Attendance.

Enrollment may be used as a historical class/section source according to Step 2H,
but a student must still be allowed to have Attendance without Enrollment.

4. FACULTY MAPPING
Attendance must NOT require Faculty Mapping, class assignment, or subject assignment.

5. SESSION
Attendance is completely Academic-Session-specific.
No calculation may silently combine multiple sessions.

6. HISTORICAL STUDENTS
Historical Attendance must remain visible even if the student:
- was promoted
- changed class/section
- is inactive now
- is absent from the current Student Registry roster
- has no current enrollment

Use the Step 2H historical roster/context authority.

7. IST
Attendance business/display time remains Asia/Kolkata / IST.

8. SCOPE
Do not fix anything unrelated to Attendance calculation unification.
