N. DELIVERY FAILURE — CRITICAL
==================================================

If email delivery fails AFTER challenge creation:

the exact newly created challenge must be invalidated.

The recovery session must be conditionally cleared using:

clearStudentPasswordRecoverySessionIfMatches(
  req,
  challengeId,
  studentId,
  schoolId
)

from Step 3B.

Do NOT use unconditional session clearing.

This is critical because another request may already have created a newer challenge.

Scenario:

A creates challenge A.

B creates challenge B.

A's email delivery fails late.

A must NOT clear B's session.

A's cleanup must return false and leave B intact.

The exact challenge invalidation must also be bound to:

challengeId
+
studentId
+
schoolId

Do not invalidate another/newer challenge.
