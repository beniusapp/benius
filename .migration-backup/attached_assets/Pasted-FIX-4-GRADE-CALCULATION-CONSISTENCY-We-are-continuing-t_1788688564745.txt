FIX 4 — GRADE CALCULATION CONSISTENCY

We are continuing the School MIS Examination calculation stabilization work.

IMPORTANT PERMANENT ARCHITECTURE RULE:

This is a TRUE MULTI-TENANT School MIS.

Tenant isolation is mandatory in every change.

A teacher/user from School A must never access, retrieve, calculate with, save, update, or infer School B's students, examination scores, academic sessions, policies, grading rules, results, attendance, faculty data, or other school-owned data.

The authenticated user's server-derived school context is authoritative.

NEVER trust a client-supplied schoolId as authorization.

Do not regress Fix 1, Fix 2, or Fix 3.

==================================================
FIX 4 GOAL
==================================================

Fix GRADE CALCULATION consistency.

The application currently has multiple places that may calculate or display grades independently.

The goal is:

ONE AUTHORITATIVE GRADE CALCULATION based on the authenticated school's configured grading policy.

The relationship must be:

student percentage
→ authenticated school's grading policy
→ authoritative grade

Pass/fail remains a SEPARATE calculation governed by Fix 3's authoritative passPercentage.

Do NOT merge grade calculation and pass/fail calculation.

==================================================
GRADING POLICY CONTRACT
==================================================

Before changing the calculation, determine from the existing schema, storage, policy UI, and current code:

1. What fields define a grading tier?
2. Is minPercent authoritative?
3. Is maxPercent authoritative?
4. Are ranges inclusive?
5. Are ranges exclusive?
6. Are gaps allowed?
7. Are overlapping ranges allowed?
8. What happens at an exact boundary?
9. Can multiple grading tiers exist for different classes?
10. Is grading policy school-scoped?
11. Is grading policy class-scoped?
12. Does the current architecture already establish an intended ordering?

Do NOT invent a new grading-policy model if the existing application already has one.

Preserve the existing intended contract unless it is clearly contradictory or unsafe.

If the repository does not provide enough evidence to determine a grading semantic, do not silently invent one.

Document the uncertainty in the final report.

==================================================
AUTHORITATIVE POLICY
==================================================

The grading policy must come from the authenticated user's school.

Use the same tenant authority established by Fix 3:

authenticated identity
→ server-derived schoolId
→ applicable class grading policy
→ grading tiers
→ grade calculation

Never:

client schoolId
→ grading policy
→ grade

Never allow a teacher from School A to calculate using School B's grading policy.

The shared calculation engine must continue enforcing its policy-school/context relationship.

If a grading policy belongs to another school, reject it.

Do not fall back to another school's policy.

Do not expose another school's policy details.

==================================================
GRADE CALCULATION
==================================================

The shared Examination calculation engine should remain the authoritative calculation layer wherever practical.

The engine must calculate the grade from the student's calculated percentage and the applicable configured grading tiers.

The grade calculation must NOT silently use a universal hardcoded grade table.

For example, if the school has configured:

90 → A+
80 → A
70 → B+
60 → B
50 → C
40 → D
0 → F

then the configured policy determines the grade.

If another school has different boundaries, those boundaries must be respected.

Do not assume 90/80/70/etc. are universal.

==================================================
MIN/MAX SEMANTICS
==================================================

If the existing grading policy explicitly defines both minPercent and maxPercent, inspect and respect that existing contract.

Be especially careful with boundaries.

Test:

- exact lower boundary
- just below lower boundary
- just above lower boundary
- exact upper boundary where applicable
- highest grade
- lowest grade

Do not introduce arbitrary floating-point behavior.

Use the application's existing percentage precision/rounding semantics unless there is a demonstrated bug directly related to grade selection.

Do NOT redesign percentage rounding in this fix.

==================================================
MISSING OR INVALID GRADING POLICY
==================================================

Do not silently fall back to:

A+ = 90
A = 80
B+ = 70
etc.

Do not silently fall back to a universal grading table.

If the required grading policy is missing or invalid, handle it as a configuration error according to the application's existing error-handling conventions.

The system must not invent a school's grading policy.

==================================================
PASS/FAIL SEPARATION
==================================================

Fix 3 established:

configured gradingTiers.passPercentage
→ authoritative Pass/Fail threshold

Fix 4 must preserve this.

Grade and Pass/Fail are separate.

Example:

passPercentage = 40%

Student percentage = 45%

The student may have:

grade = D
passed = true

if the school's configured grading policy produces that result.

Do not make grade boundaries determine Pass/Fail.

Do not make passPercentage determine grade.
