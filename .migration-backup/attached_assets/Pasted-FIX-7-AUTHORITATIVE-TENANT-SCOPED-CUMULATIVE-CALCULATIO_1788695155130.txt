=============================================================

IMPORTANT — READ THIS FIRST
=============================================================

This application is a TRUE MULTI-TENANT SAAS PLATFORM designed for 1,000+ independent schools to register and use the same application.

Each school is an independent TENANT.

This is NOT one universal School MIS policy shared by all schools.

Every school may configure its own academic/examination policies.

Therefore:

SCHOOL A may have one cumulative calculation policy.
SCHOOL B may have a completely different cumulative calculation policy.
SCHOOL C may have another policy.
SCHOOL D may have another policy.
And so on for 1,000+ schools.

The application MUST calculate cumulative results according to the policy belonging to the authenticated/current tenant.

There must NEVER be a universal cumulative formula silently applied to every school.

Tenant isolation is permanent and non-negotiable.

=============================================================
FIX 7 OBJECTIVE
=============================================================

Implement:

FIX 7 — AUTHORITATIVE, TENANT-SCOPED CUMULATIVE CALCULATION

The purpose of this fix is to establish one authoritative cumulative/year-end calculation interpretation while allowing EACH SCHOOL/TENANT to use its own configured cumulative policy.

The system must determine:

Authenticated tenant
        ↓
That tenant's applicable cumulative policy
        ↓
That tenant's validated Academic Session
        ↓
That student's valid term results
        ↓
That tenant's cumulative calculation
        ↓
Cumulative result

DO NOT create one global cumulative formula for all schools.

=============================================================
CRITICAL RULE — SCHOOL-SPECIFIC POLICY
=============================================================

The most important requirement in this fix is:

CUMULATIVE CALCULATION IS TENANT-SPECIFIC.

For example:

School A could configure:

Term 1 = 20%
Term 2 = 30%
Term 3 = 50%

School B could configure:

Term 1 = 25%
Term 2 = 25%
Term 3 = 25%
Term 4 = 25%

School C could configure:

Term 1 = 40%
Term 2 = 60%

These are examples only.

DO NOT create these policies automatically.

The actual application's existing configuration/schema must determine what is possible.

The calculation engine must use the policy belonging to the authenticated tenant.

School A's policy MUST NOT be used for School B.

School B's policy MUST NOT be used for School C.

No school may inherit another school's cumulative policy.

=============================================================
AUTHORITATIVE TENANT FLOW
=============================================================

Cumulative calculation MUST follow:

Authenticated user
        ↓
Authenticated user's schoolId / tenant
        ↓
Validated Academic Session belonging to that tenant
        ↓
Applicable cumulative policy belonging to that tenant
        ↓
Student belonging to that tenant
        ↓
Term results belonging to that tenant + session
        ↓
Authoritative cumulative calculation

The authenticated server-side tenant is authoritative.

A client-supplied schoolId MUST NEVER select cumulative policy.

A client-supplied policy ID MUST NOT bypass tenant ownership validation.

A client-supplied term result or cumulative value MUST NOT become authoritative.
