We are investigating whether the application's Academic Policy / Grading system can safely support DECIMAL grading boundaries such as:

A: 80.00–100.00
B: 60.00–79.99
C: 35.00–59.99
D: 0.00–34.99

The immediate business requirement is that a calculated result such as 59.7% should be able to receive grade C instead of throwing:

"No configured grading rule matches percentage 59.7."

The previous investigation already established that the current system converts "59.99" to integer 59 in the Principal Portal and that the database columns min_percent/max_percent are currently integer types.

Your task is ONLY to investigate the complete technical impact of changing the grading-boundary contract from INTEGER percentages to DECIMAL percentages.
