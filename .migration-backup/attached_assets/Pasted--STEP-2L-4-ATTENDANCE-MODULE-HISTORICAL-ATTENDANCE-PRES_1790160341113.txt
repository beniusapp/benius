# STEP 2L-4 — ATTENDANCE MODULE

## HISTORICAL ATTENDANCE PRESERVATION AFTER PHYSICAL STUDENT DELETION

### DEEP AUDIT + CONTROLLED IMPLEMENTATION

We have completed and accepted:

* Step 2L-1 — Server-authoritative Academic Session selection for Teacher Attendance writes.
* Step 2L-2 — Academic Session date-boundary enforcement.
* Step 2L-3 — Student Attendance marked-Sunday display and monthly-count consistency.

Now address the remaining critical Attendance issue:

> **Physical deletion of a Student currently deletes historical Student Attendance because `attendance_records.student_id` uses `ON DELETE CASCADE`.**

Our required business behavior is:

> **Historical Attendance must survive even if the Student Registry record is physically deleted.**

This is a data-integrity requirement.

This step must be handled very carefully.

---

# 1. NON-NEGOTIABLE BUSINESS REQUIREMENT

Student Registry is GLOBAL/PERMANENT.

However:

### Historical Attendance is permanent historical data.

If a Student is physically deleted from the global Student Registry:

```text
Student Registry record
        ↓
PHYSICALLY DELETED
```

historical Attendance records must NOT be deleted.

For example:

```text
Student ID: STU123
Academic Session: 2025-26
Attendance:
  10-Apr-2025 Present
  11-Apr-2025 Absent
  12-Apr-2025 Present
```

If the Student Registry record is later physically deleted:

```text
STU123
```

the historical Attendance rows must remain.

The system must still be able to display historical Attendance for that deleted Student when the relevant historical Attendance is accessed through the appropriate existing historical workflow.

---

# 2. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Do NOT interpret this requirement as:

> "Never delete students."

That is NOT the requirement.

The Student Registry may continue to support its existing physical deletion behavior if that behavior is intentionally supported by the application.

The requirement is:

> **Deleting the Student Registry identity must not destroy historical Attendance data.**

Do not remove or disable Student deletion unless absolutely necessary and explicitly reported.

---

# 3. HARD REQUIREMENTS — DO NOT CHANGE

## Multi-tenant isolation

Every Attendance record remains strictly school/tenant-specific.

Never allow:

* one school's Student to access another school's Attendance;
* one school's historical Attendance to appear in another school;
* a deleted Student identity to be reused across schools in a way that causes historical data leakage.

Authenticated school context remains authoritative.

---

## Academic Session isolation

Every Student Attendance record must remain tied to its Academic Session.

Historical Attendance must continue to be isolated by:

```text
school + academic session
```

Do not remove the Attendance Session relationship.

Do not make Attendance global across Sessions.

---

## Student Registry

Student Registry remains global/permanent.

Do NOT:

* recreate students per Academic Session;
* make Enrollment mandatory for Attendance;
* copy the entire Student record into every Session;
* redesign Student Registry.

---

## Existing Attendance behavior

Do not change:

* Teacher Attendance marking;
* Step 2L-1 server-authoritative Session resolution;
* Step 2L-2 Session date boundaries;
* 7-day correction window;
* archived Session protection;
* Sunday behavior;
* holiday rules;
* Attendance status semantics;
* Student Attendance monthly calculation;
* Admin Attendance behavior unless required to preserve historical records.

# 7. HISTORICAL IDENTITY REQUIREMENT

This is a critical part of the fix.

If the Student Registry row is physically deleted, historical Attendance still needs a usable identity.

At minimum, historical Attendance must retain enough information to identify the Student for historical display/reporting.

Do NOT rely on the current `students` row existing forever.

The implementation must answer:

> "If `students.id` no longer exists, how does the historical Attendance row know who it belonged to?"

Inspect whether the current Attendance schema already contains any historical identity fields such as:

* student name;
* admission/roll number;
* registration number;
* student code;
* other immutable identity.

Do not assume these fields exist.

---

# 8. IMPORTANT: DO NOT COPY CURRENT MUTABLE STUDENT DATA BLINDLY

Do NOT solve the problem by simply copying every current Student field into Attendance.

We need historical identity, not a duplicate Student Registry.

Identify the minimum immutable/display identity required by existing Attendance screens.

For example, depending on the actual schema, this could be something equivalent to:

```text
student_id
historical_student_name
historical_admission_number
```

But this is ONLY an example.

Use the actual project schema and existing identity model.

Do not invent unnecessary fields.

---

# 9. CHOOSE THE SAFEST DESIGN BASED ON AUDIT

After the audit, determine the smallest safe implementation.

Possible approaches may include:

### Option A — Preserve Student FK but prevent physical deletion

Only acceptable if the application explicitly requires Students to remain undeletable.

However, this conflicts with the current requirement that physical deletion may occur, so do NOT choose this simply because it is easier.

### Option B — Change Attendance FK delete behavior and preserve historical identity separately

For example:

```text
student deleted
        ↓
Attendance remains
        ↓
student_id becomes NULL or otherwise preserved
        +
historical identity remains stored
```

This may be appropriate if the existing architecture supports nullable historical references.

### Option C — Historical Attendance snapshot identity

Attendance keeps its original immutable identity information so it can render even when the Student Registry row disappears.

This may be appropriate if existing Attendance UI depends heavily on Student joins.

### Other approach

If the audit identifies a safer existing architecture, use it.

# 22. CRITICAL SCOPE CONTROL

This is a sensitive data-integrity change.

Do NOT perform a broad refactor.

Do NOT redesign the Student Registry.

Do NOT redesign Enrollment.

Do NOT redesign Attendance.

Do NOT introduce a new Attendance architecture.

Do NOT modify unrelated modules.

Do NOT delete or rewrite existing historical Attendance.

Do NOT "solve" the issue by simply disabling Student deletion.

The target behavior is:

```text
BEFORE

Student Registry
      ↓ physical delete
students row deleted
      ↓ CASCADE
historical Attendance deleted ❌


AFTER

Student Registry
      ↓ physical delete
students row deleted
      ↓
historical Attendance preserved ✅
      ↓
historical identity preserved ✅
      ↓
original Academic Session preserved ✅
      ↓
tenant isolation preserved ✅
```

Most importantly:

> **Historical Attendance is more authoritative than the current existence of the Student Registry row.**

A Student being removed from the current Registry must never destroy historical Attendance that has already been recorded.

Do not implement anything beyond this requirement in Step 2L-4.
