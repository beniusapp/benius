---

Important Distinction

UPI / QR Payment is NOT Razorpay Online Payment.

UPI / QR Payment means:

«Parent/student pays directly to the school's own UPI/QR account → admin verifies the transaction → admin records it manually.»

Razorpay means:

«Parent/student uses the application's existing Razorpay checkout → Razorpay handles the payment → existing webhook/payment verification system records it.»

Keep these two systems completely separate.

Security

Maintain the existing strict multi-tenant "school_id" isolation.

An admin must only be able to record a UPI / QR payment against an invoice belonging to their own school.

Do not weaken any existing invoice validation, duplicate-payment protection, authorization, or server-side verification.
