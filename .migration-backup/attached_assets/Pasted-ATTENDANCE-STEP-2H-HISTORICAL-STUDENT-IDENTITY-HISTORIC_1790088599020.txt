3. CRITICAL DISTINCTION — ENROLLMENT IS NOT AN ATTENDANCE GATE
==================================================

This is extremely important.

Existing Attendance records are authoritative historical records.

Session-specific Enrollment may be used to determine the historical
class/section/roster CONTEXT where appropriate.

BUT:

An Attendance record MUST NOT disappear merely because a corresponding
Enrollment row does not exist.

Do NOT change the Attendance marking rule to require Enrollment.

Do NOT reject Attendance because a student has no Enrollment.

Do NOT make Attendance visibility depend exclusively on Enrollment.

The following must remain possible:

    Attendance record exists
    +
    valid school_id
    +
    valid session_id
    +
    valid student_id

Even if no Enrollment row is found, the Attendance record itself must
remain readable when the authenticated user is authorized to view that
school/session.

Enrollment is historical context where available, NOT the authoritative
existence condition for Attendance.
